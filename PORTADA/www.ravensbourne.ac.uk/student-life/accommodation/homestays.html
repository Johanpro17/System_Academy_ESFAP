Homestays

You are in the section

  • Home
  • Student life
  • Accommodation

Homestay offers a practical and financially savvy accommodation option for Ravensbourne University students. Rather than rushing into expensive year-long contracts for student halls, the homestay experience provides significant cost savings and flexibility while you find your feet in London.

Homestay is also a good option for students who will be under 18-years-old on the first day of their course.

Global Guardians

Become a live-in property guardian, or a long-term house sitter, in one of many empty buildings all across London. The properties have been restored by Global Guardians to be rented out at affordable prices.
