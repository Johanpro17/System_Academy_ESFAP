Capital gains

  1. One of the safest cities in the world 
  2. Iconic building designed to inspire creative minds 
  3. Excellent transport links 
  4. Diverse city teeming with creativity and culture 
  5. Industry-leading facilities and equipment

London life

You want your child to broaden their horizons and find a home away from home. At Ravensbourne, they will collaborate with likeminded creatives from over 90 different countries in our award-winning creative hub. Within its walls lives a community teeming with energy and creativity. 

It’s impossible for your child not to be inspired by the passionate minds, bright lights and rich culture that surround them, allowing them to reach their full potential as learners, workers and global citizens. Your child will have the freedom to be themselves, find their unique voice and fulfil their creative destiny.

Exceptional transport links will not only give them easy access to a tremendous city, it will also make it easier for you to reach them. Hop on the Jubilee Line from North Greenwich to Oxford Street and they’ll be in central London in just 20 minutes, where they can sample the city's rich heritage.  

There’s a bustling social and entertainment scene too. Westfield Shopping Centre, the largest shopping centre in Europe, is just 10 minutes away, while there is a plethora of art galleries, theatres, live music events, exhibitions and museums. The only drawback is: when you visit them, you won't want to leave! 

Our locale

Our locale

Within Ravensbourne's immediate vicinity, you will find a wealth of food places, modern technology and illustrious shops and brands. The world-famous O2 Arena is literally next door. It’s not just a music venue either; Ravensbourne students get 20% off a diverse range of eateries inside the building.

Many students live along the riverside in North Greenwich and we work with several accommodation partners to help our students find a great place to live.

Award-winning building 

On the Greenwich Peninsula and swathed in a mosaic of 28,000 gleaming tiles, our home makes a real statement – just like our students. 

Designed by Farshid Moussavi in 2010, the Ravensbourne building has been hailed as a masterclass in functional design and public art, claiming the RIBA award for best Higher Education building in 2011.

Campus life 

The Ravensbourne Student Union (RSU) is the mouthpiece for our students in the local area, representing them on all matters affecting life and education, whilst helping to manage a packed calendar of welfare, social, cultural and sporting activities.

Equipped to deliver

Equipped to deliver

Your child will not only have access to the leading tutors and industry connections at Ravensbourne, they will also be granted use of cutting-edge facilities and equipment designed to fulfil the needs of tomorrow’s creative.  

Whether it’s our TV studio or fashion atelier, prototyping suite or 3D printer, Ravensbourne students have some of the finest creative facilities available.

Not only is it really fun but it brings you up to speed on modern technology you may encounter in industry.”
