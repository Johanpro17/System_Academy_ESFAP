Our manifesto

Creative Lab is an agency, a studio, a network and a community. 

We challenge established thinking to understand emerging culture. 

We explore, analyse, define, test and refine to shape strategy, organise events, create products,  design services and facilitate creative communication.

We deliver impactful solutions through shared intelligence & collaboration. 

Our work

What we do?

Creative Lab is the space where industry and education can discover together.

We work with a community of alumni, students, practitioners and industry partners to provide creative services for brands and public institutions.

We make films, curate events, prototype digital products, facilitate insight and design communication strategies.

Over the last five years we have worked global brands such as Apple, Nike, Barclays, Deloitte, Kopparberg and Atlantic and public organisations like the Obama Foundation and the NHS.

We connect to the next generation of creative leaders to challenge convention and deliver impactful solutions. Through this shared intelligence we connect to emerging cultures to understand new technologies and analyse developing trends.  

hide

Why we do it?

Industry collaboration is part of the DNA at Ravensbourne. Our aim is to ‘learn with industry’ and we are pioneering a unique approach that is designed to drive innovation and support employability.

Creative Lab is a key driver for this approach and sits at the heart of an integrated network of student support hubs designed to build professional skills and support our professional life practice modules.

How can students get involved?

All students are welcome to work with Creative Lab. If you are keen to get involved, you can find more information on the Creative Lab AULA page. 

We regularly work with students as part of our Creative Lab internship scheme and all interns are paid the London Living Wage.

Please contact Derek Yates if you have any questions about getting involved.
