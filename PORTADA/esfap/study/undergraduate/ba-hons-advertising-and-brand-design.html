The specifics

  • Creative Strategy
  • Branding and Identity Design
  • Copywriting and Art Direction
  • Campaign Development
  • Team-working and Project Management

If you study the BA (Hons) Advertising and Brand Design with Integrated Foundation Year version of this course, key study topics in your foundation year will include:

  • Fundamental design principles: typography, color, hierarchy, layout, and imagery
  • Developing clear visual narratives, identities, and design systems
  • Crafting effective visual storytelling through sequencing, pace, and tone
  • Conducting research with cultural, social, and ethical awareness
  • Mastery of industry-standard software (Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign, Animate, After Effects, Acrobat, Premiere Pro)
  • Managing production workflows from pre-production to post-production
  • Critically evaluating design work using relevant criteria
  • Creating professional portfolios, slide decks, and pitch presentations

If you choose to add an Integrated Foundation Year on this course, you will be first introduced to the fundamental principles of design. Your foundation year gives you the time, space and support to explore your strengths, build confidence and prepare for the demands of project-based, industry-focused learning. Modules are as follows*:

  • Module 1: Diagnostic exploration - making informed choices
  • Module 2: Technical - Technical knowledge in practice
  • Module 3: Specialist - Innovating specialist practice
  • Module 4: Integration - Integrated practice for progression to Higher Education

Please note: Students starting this course with the Integrated Foundation Year in September 2026/27 will study the 2027/28 approved modules in Years 1, 2 and 3.

Year 1

Discover contemporary branding and advertising by building essential creative, strategic and technical skills. Learn to generate innovative ideas, solve complex design problems and explore emerging industry practices.

Modules may include:

  • Rave Beginnings
  • Creative Thinking & Process
  • Fundamentals of Practice
  • Strategy, Brand Building & Communication
  • Campaign Communication
  • Employability & Professionalism

Year 2

In Year 2 you will challenge design conventions through cross-disciplinary collaboration, immersive brand building and storytelling. You will also have the chance to secure a real-world experience live work placement backed by leading partners like Sky, Barclays, JKR, Concept Kicks and Uncommon Creative Studio.

Modules may include:

  • Immersive Worlds
  • Preparing for Industry
  • Audience Insight and Storytelling
  • Design Studio
  • Innovation Sprints
  • Live & Work-based Learning

Year 3

Tackle global competition briefs and ambitious, purpose-led projects to highlight your strategic thinking. Build an industry-ready professional portfolio that defines your personal brand and secures your path into advertising and design.

Modules may include:

  • Industry Collaboration
  • Situating Practice
  • Competition Briefs
  • Professional Practice Futures
  • Major Project

A variety of learning and teaching methods are used on this course.

These include but are not limited to:

Briefings, lectures, project work, seminars, workshops, collaborative work, individual and group tutorials, group presentations, critiques, external visits, industry guest lectures, studio visits, industry-informed briefs, gallery visits.

The course is assessed entirely by coursework - this includes practical outcomes, portfolios, written and reflective work, pitches and presentations.

Our campus 

You'll study in a building designed to encourage collaboration between the different disciplines and practices within Ravensbourne, and to stimulate the work of innovative professionals and creative collaboration.

It features a variety of workspaces, including social and quiet areas, high-tech creative suites, technology hubs, design studios and more.

Ravensbourne is ideally connected - North Greenwich station is just a 2-minute walk away from our campus and offers amazing views from the Greenwich Peninsula.

These transport links offer an easy route to some of London's major business hubs, perfect for reaching possible placements or networking events with employers in the worlds of business or technology.  

Entry requirements

At Ravensbourne we accept a wide range of qualifications for entry onto our courses, whilst also considering the context in which they were achieved. For this course, we would usually require a GCSE Grade 4 or above in English in addition to any of the below;

  • Three A Levels at grade CCC or above
  • BTEC Extended Diploma at grade MMM
  • UAL Level 3 Extended Diploma
  • T Level (pass or above)
  • Access to Higher Education Diploma
  • International Baccalaureate at grade 25 or above
  • Other/Mixed qualifications equivalent to 96 UCAS Tariff points

At Ravensbourne we take a flexible approach to admissions, as we want you to know exactly what you need to achieve in your exams to guarantee a place on the course. We still think exams are important and look forward to hearing about your successes, but we place just as much importance on the work you will share with us as part of our portfolio process.

Portfolio and interview requirements

Admission onto this course is also subject to successful completion of a portfolio (or showreel). 

If you wish to study our BA (Hons) Advertising and Brand Design degree, we would like to see examples of your advertising, design, brand and/or marketing projects that you have produced during previous academic, professional or personal pursuits.

For this course you will need to provide a portfolio of material comprising of work created during your previous creative education, alongside any personal or professional projects.

This might include:

  • Typography
  • Graphic design
  • Adverts
  • Posters
  • Digital design
  • Animation
  • Photography
  • Written material such as scripts or copywriting.

It is also important that you include research and development material such as moodboards, experiments, tests, thumbnails and brainstorms.

We do not expect a perfect professional finish but would rather see that you are prepared to take risks and explore outside your comfort zone.
