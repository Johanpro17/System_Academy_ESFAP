More reasons to choose this course

This digital marketing course, with access to state-of-the-art facilities, gives you a comprehensive overview of the digital landscape and the tools to connect businesses with customers.

It prepares you for the industry by blending real-life experience with academic rigor, whether you want to work for an agency, in-house, freelance, or run your own business.

You’ll learn to plan and execute omnichannel campaigns across digital platforms, including search engines, social media, and email. Throughout your studies, you’ll focus on digital marketing concepts, technologies, and develop the skills to design, deliver, and evaluate end-to-end campaigns.

Professionally accredited course

You will have the opportunity to gain professional qualifications through an Accredited Degree partnership with The Chartered Institute of Marketing (CIM). CIM qualifications are highly sought after by employers, and their content is reflected in our degrees, equipping you with the best opportunities for a successful marketing career.

  • Digital marketing basics

If you choose to add an Integrated Foundation Year on this course, you will start with an introduction to the fundamental principles of studying and working in the creative industries. Your foundation year gives you the time, space and support to explore your strengths, build confidence and prepare for the demands of project-based, industry-focused learning.  

Modules include: 

  • Critical & Creative Thinking  

  • Academic & Professional Writing & Communication  

  • Contexts of Creative Industries  

  • Digital Literacies & Skills 

  • Contemporary Issues & Culture 

  • Creative Ventures 

Please note: Students starting this course with the Integrated Foundation Year in September 2026/27 will study the 2027/28 approved modules in Years 1, 2 and 3.

Year 1

Gain hands-on skills in designing user-focused web experiences , digital advertising, and analytics while mastering core marketing principles, sales practice and e-commerce.

Modules may include:

  • Rave Beginnings
  • Creative Marketing Case Studies
  • Markets, Customers and Sales Practice
  • E-Commerce and Digital Experience Design
  • Digital Ads and Analytics
  • Industry Skills for Marketing

Year 2

Develop your expertise in customer journey mapping, brand communication, visual creation and strategic storytelling for modern marketing campaigns.

Modules may include:

  • Branding and Communication Design
  • Photography and Visual Creation
  • Consumer Journey
  • Digital Brand Analysis
  • Storytelling and Marketing Campaigns
  • Strategic and Contextual Marketing

Year 3

Hone your strategic focus with a closer look at social media marketing, emerging ad tech and take on the challenges of industry consultancy projects and an independent research dissertation.

Modules may include:

  • Social Media Marketing for Industry
  • Emerging Technologies in Marketing
  • Consultancy Project
  • Marketing Research and Methods
  • Preparing for Industry and the Future of Marketing
  • Individual Project: Dissertation, Industry or Work-Based Research Project

A variety of learning and teaching methods are employed in this course. 

These include but are not limited to;

Briefings, lectures, project work, seminars, workshops, collaborative work, online activity, individual tutorials, group presentations, critiques, external visits, technical support and self-directed independent study.

The course is assessed entirely by coursework.

Our campus 

You'll study in a building designed to encourage collaboration between the different disciplines and practices within Ravensbourne, and to stimulate the work of innovative professionals and creative collaboration.

It features a variety of workspaces, including social and quiet areas, high-tech creative suites, technology hubs, design studios and more. 

Want to see where you'd be studying up close? When you book a guided building tour, you can see our fantastic facilities for yourself and find out more from one of our helpful student ambassadors.

Our local area

Ravensbourne is ideally connected - North Greenwich station is just a 2-minute walk away from our campus and offers amazing views from the Greenwich Peninsula.

These transport links offer an easy route to some of London's major business hubs, perfect for reaching possible placements or networking events with employers in the worlds of business or technology.  

Entry requirements

At Ravensbourne we accept a wide range of qualifications for entry onto our courses, whilst also considering the context in which they were achieved. For this course, we would usually require a GCSE Grade 4 or above in English in addition to any of the below;

  • Three A Levels at grade BBC or above
  • BTEC Extended Diploma at grade DMM
  • UAL Level 3 Extended Diploma
  • T Level (pass or above)
  • Access to Higher Education Diploma
  • International Baccalaureate at grade 25 or above
  • Other/Mixed qualifications equivalent to 112 UCAS Tariff points

There are no portfolio requirements for this course.
