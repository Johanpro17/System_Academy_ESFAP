More reasons to choose this course

Our BA (Hons) Fashion Business, Brand and Marketing degree in London prepares you for a career in fashion buying and brand management, offering you an understanding of most head office roles whilst giving you flexibility to switch career paths as required.

The course focuses on industry-style reporting rather than sketchbooks or sewing, giving you practical knowledge of product development, including fabric selection and testing, colour management, quality control and sustainability. CAD and CAD/CAM tools support creative presentation and industry preparation. You will also get the chance to experience specialist career events with tailored fashion brands such as Next, Whistles and Hobbs.

With a strong focus on responsible thinking, diverse perspectives and future-facing innovation, you’ll graduate with an enviable set of transferable skills that balance creativity, analysis , people and planet. 

Professionally accredited course

Expected professional accreditation: CIM

You will have the opportunity to gain professional qualifications through an Accredited Degree partnership with The Chartered Institute of Marketing (CIM). CIM qualifications are highly sought after by employers, and their content is reflected in our degrees, equipping you with the best opportunities for a successful marketing career.

The specifics

Your learning is enriched through diverse guest speakers, industry led projects and visits that provide behind the scenes access to manufacturers and retail spaces. Using the Adobe suite and industry resources, you’ll develop the creative and practical skills to analyse trends and data, identify your consumer, plan a fashion range or store layout, promote a brand, produce a professional report, pitch an idea and understand the business of fashion. 

  • Trend forecasting 
  • Consumer profiling and identification 
  • Fashion market research 
  • Fashion buying and product development
  • Merchandising and visual merchandising 
  • Sourcing and sustainability 
  • Fashion brand management 
  • Marketing a brand
  • Business planning 

If you are studying the BA (Hons) Fashion Business, Brand and Marketing with Integrated Foundation Year version of this course, key topics of your integrated foundation year will include:

  • Basics of fashion promotion, buying, and brand communications
  • Conceptualisation and direction in fashion design
  • Fashion illustration and life drawing techniques
  • 3D draping and garment construction on the stand
  • Techniques in fabric manipulation and digital printing
  • Fashion styling and photography
  • Brand identity management and creative leadership
  • Prototyping, atelier practices, and digital tool usage
  • Understanding industry trends and audience expectations

If you choose to add an Integrated Foundation Year on this course, you will be first introduced to the fundamental principles of fashion. Your foundation year gives you the time, space and support to explore your strengths, build confidence and prepare for the demands of project-based, industry-focused learning. Modules are as follows:

  • Module 1: Diagnostic exploration - making informed choices
  • Module 2: Technical - Technical knowledge in practice
  • Module 3: Specialist - Innovating specialist practice
  • Module 4: Integration - Integrated practice for progression to Higher Education

Please note: Students starting this course with the Integrated Foundation Year in September 2026/27 will study the 2027/28 approved modules in Years 1, 2 and 3.

Year 1

Develop skills in trend forecasting, customer profiling, fashion buying, marketing and digital brand communication while defining your professional fashion industry identity.

Modules may include: 

  • Fashion Fundamentals
  • The Industry
  • Now: Trends, Communities and Behaviours
  • The Buy: Product, Place, Performance
  • Introduction to Brand and Marketing 
  • Your Path: Progress, Potential, Plan

Year 2

Master responsible sourcing, brand promotion, brand marketing and immersive retail merchandising through cross-disciplinary industry briefs and hands-on work placements.

  • Industry & Collaboration
  • Marketing Strategy: Brand Building and Promotion
  • Career Catalyst
  • Future: Trends, Communities and Behaviour
  • Immersive Retail
  • Industry Experience

Year 3

Identify a market gap and design a complete brand launch plan using deep market research, industry briefs and your own creativity. Graduate job-ready by taking part in mock industry inverviews and mock industry assessment centres.

Modules may include: 

  • Sprints and Ideation
  • White Space: Market, Consumer Competitor
  • Your Future: Next Steps, Networking, New Start
  • Final Major Project
  • Final Major Project Pitch

A variety of learning and teaching methods are used on this course.

These include but are not limited to:

Briefings, lectures, seminars, practical workshops, demonstrations, collaborative activities, group discussions, individual and group tutorials, critiques, presentations, industry guest lectures, visits, industry-informed briefs, field trips, gallery visits and self-directed independent study.

The course is assessed entirely by coursework - this includes practical outcomes, portfolios, written and reflective work, pitches and presentations.

Our students benefit from ongoing partnerships with leading fashion brands. In 2025, for the second year running, third-year students took part in an Early Careers Day at the NEXT head office, gaining first-hand insight into fashion buying, merchandising and large-scale retail operations. They also worked on a live project developing strategic marketing strategies for beauty platform Upper Canada, experimenting with AI to generate realistic visual outcomes. 

Current alumni are working across the industry - from in-house buying teams and freelance content creation to running their own brands - at companies including Burberry, Me+Em, URBN, NEXT, ASOS, Selfridges, Harrods, Rapha, M&S, Boden, TFG, Dune, Adidas, Odd Muse, WGSN, Sweaty Betty, PVH, UGG and Coach.

Our campus 

You'll study in a building designed to encourage collaboration between the different disciplines and practices within Ravensbourne, and to stimulate the work of innovative professionals and creative collaboration.

It features a variety of workspaces, including social and quiet areas, high-tech creative suites, technology hubs, design studios and more. 

Want to see where you'd be studying up close? When you book a guided building tour, you can see our fantastic facilities for yourself and find out more from one of our helpful student ambassadors.

Our local area

Ravensbourne is ideally connected - North Greenwich station is just a 2-minute walk away from our campus and offers amazing views from the Greenwich Peninsula.

These transport links offer an easy route to some of London's major business hubs, perfect for reaching possible placements or networking events with employers in the worlds of business or technology.  

Entry requirements

We accept a wide range of qualifications for entry onto our courses, whilst also considering the context in which they were achieved. For this course, we would usually require a GCSE Grade 4 or above in English and Maths in addition to any of the below;

  • Three A Levels at grade BCC or above
  • BTEC Extended Diploma at grade DMM
  • UAL Level 3 Extended Diploma
  • T Level (pass or above)
  • Access to Higher Education Diploma
  • International Baccalaureate at grade 25 or above
  • Other/Mixed qualifications equivalent to 112 UCAS Tariff points

There are no portfolio requirements for this course.
