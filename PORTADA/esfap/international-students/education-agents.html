The UK Agent Quality Framework (AQF)

We have joined a group of universities in setting industry standards in international student recruitment and committed to the UK Agent Quality Framework (AQF). The AQF represents a significant step towards maintaining the highest standards of agent management, ensuring that the UK remains a premier study destination for international students. It reflects our dedication to sustainable internationalisation and highlights our proactive approach to agent quality assurance.

By signing the pledge, we are reinforcing our commitment to delivering a quality experience for our students and partners and we have pledge to:

  • Empower Student Choice: Ensuring informed decision-making and transparency.
  • Maintain High Standards: Committing to agent governance and professionalism.
  • Promote Ethical Practices: Upholding ethical agent practices and professional competency.
  • Ensure Accountability: Increasing transparency and accountability across the board.
