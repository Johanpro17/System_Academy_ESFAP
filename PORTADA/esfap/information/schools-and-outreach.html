Design your future

We have designed our outreach programme to give young people real insight into what it’s like to study at university level. We offer creative activities, pre-university advice and much more.

Teachers and career advisers

We work with teachers and careers advisers to help students explore higher education and career options in the creative and digital industries. 

We support schools and colleges through:

  • Campus visits and subject workshops
  • In-school talks and presentations
  • Portfolio and personal statement support
  • Creative careers workshops
  • Free digital resources

Browse our offering below and email [email protected] to request a session.

  • Discover Ravensbourne 
  • Careers in the Creative Industries
  • Student Life 

Discover Ravensbourne: explores undergraduate courses at Ravensbourne and how to apply. Students gain knowledge of Ravensbourne and an understanding of the UCAS application process.

Careers in the Creative Industries: learners can participate in activities to learn about future careers in the creative and technology industries. 

Student Life: A Ravensbourne student will share their experience of student life and what studying certain subjects at university is really like. We can tailor subject studied to best suit your cohort

Duration: 1 hour each 

Audience: Most suited for 14 years+ students

Location: In-school or incorporated into a campus visit

Gatsby benchmarks: G1,G2,G3,G4,G5,G7,G8

Portofolio and Showreel Advice: a presentation with tips for making the best possible portfolio or showreel for application to creative courses at university. After the presentation, we can review individual portfolios and give learners 1-1 feedback and share creative project briefs to help develop ideas further. 

Personal Statement Clinic: a presentation with tips and advice on how to write a successful personal statement. Students will learn what makes a strong statement and how to put this into practice. We can then review personal statements and give learners 1-1 feedback. 

Duration

This is a 1-hour session (longer may be needed depending on number of 1:1 reviews) 

Audience 

Most suited for students who have started making their UCAS applications. 

Where

In-School 

Gatsby benchmarks

G1, G2, G3, G4, G5 G7, G8

In collaboration with our academic teams we can deliver subject-specific workshops on campus that give students a taste of what studying at Ravensbourne will be like.

These include fashion masterclasses and TV studio days that immerse students in hands-on, practical projects. 

Student Ambassadors will take your students on a tour of our campus and facilities. 

Duration

Ranges from half to full day 

Audience

Most suited for 16 year old + students

Gatsby benchmarks

G1, G3, G7, G8

Inspire your students at our End of Year Degree Show, where final-year students showcase their major projects.

Groups will be able to:

  1. play student-created games
  2. watch short films
  3. view fashion and design work
  4. explore projects across our creative courses

Students will also take part in a workshop inspired by the exhibition.

Suitable for: students aged 16+ interested in exploring creative degree courses.

Bring your students to Ravensbourne University in North Greenwich for a one-day, hands-on workshop. Students will work in groups with a Ravensbourne academic to design and pitch a technological solution to an everyday problem.

The session helps students develop:

  1. research and idea development skills
  2. pitching and presentation skills
  3. confidence and teamwork

Suitable for: students aged 16+ interested in creative, business and technology subjects.

This programme offers tailored guidance and encouragement to rebuild confidence and ensure a smooth transition into university life

Through this programme, mature applicants will be able to:

  1. Explore their options
  2. Build confidence
  3. Develop practical digital skills
  4. Master work-life balance
  5. Connect with peers

Suitable for: Individuals looking to access higher education, aged 21+ or over. More information can be found here.

Meet the team

We are a team passionate about making learning and teaching easier, more engaging, and downright enjoyable. Whether sharing resources, offering guidance, or just answering your questions, we are here to support you every step of the way.

Alice - UK Student Recruitment Manager

I lead the UK Student Recruitment Team's operations, partnerships with schools and colleges, oversight of the student ambassador programme, and the development of our on-campus school engagement offer. I also represent Ravensbourne at events and deliver workshops to help prospective students explore higher education.

Hattie - Progression and Outreach Manager

I lead Ravensbourne's Access Intervention Strategy as part of our Access and Participation Plan, with the aim to make studying at Ravensbourne more equitable. My team and I work with schools, colleges and community groups to run a wide range of projects to increase the number of students from underrepresented backgrounds who progress to higher education.

Helena - UK Student Recruitment Officer

I support the university’s student recruitment activity by engaging with schools and colleges, coordinating on-campus visits, information sessions, and representing the university at recruitment events. I deliver presentations and workshops, on and off campus, manage some event logistics and bookings, and support the smooth running of on-campus student recruitment activities.

Floss - UK Student Recruitment Officer

I’m part of Ravensbourne’s Student Recruitment Team and work closely with schools and colleges to help prospective students explore higher education. I deliver presentations and workshops, represent the university at events, and help organise on-campus visits and wider recruitment activities.

Kim - Outreach Officer

I work to support the Progression and Outreach Team, focusing on the engagement of schools and colleges. I’m also the main point of contact for Primary and Pre-16 projects that we offer. I deliver and facilitate practical workshops and talks, alongside representing Ravensbourne at HE Fairs and supporting the Student Recruitment Team at UCAS exhibitions.

Nadia - Outreach Officer

I work to support our Progression and Outreach Team. I help to facilitate engagement of schools and colleges and I’m also the main point of contact for community engagement. I also foster relationships with non-profits and other charitable organisations to support marginalised groups in higher education settings.

Become a partner

Our primary objective is to support the next generation to progress and succeed within the creative and digital industries.

Each year we attend a range of events to support our partners:

  • HE Fairs, including school and college events and UCAS exhibitions
  • Group visits and discovery days on campus
  • Tours of our Degree Show
  • Invitations to attend on campus industry events 

If you're interested in setting up a partnership with us, please get in touch.

Our partnership offer can provide:

  • Talks and presentations from our academics and student ambassadors, covering: our course offer, the creative industries and graduate/alumni stories, student life and advice on how to apply.
  • Pre application workshops for post 16 students, covering: personal statements, portfolio and showreel surgeries and mock interview practice.
  • Subject specific workshops and masterclasses
  • Holiday schools at Easter and Summer
  • After School and Saturday Clubs
  • CPD opportunities for teaching and careers staff
